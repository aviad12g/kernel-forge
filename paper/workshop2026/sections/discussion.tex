\section{Discussion}

\paragraph{Relation to prior work.}
KernelBench measures kernel generation at scale~\citep{kernelbench2025}.
KernelBench-Verified and robust-kbench strengthen hidden tests, precision
settings, memory analysis, and verification scenarios
~\citep{zhang2026kernelbenchverified,lange2025robustkbench}. FastKernels studies
alignment with production workloads~\citep{oliaro2026fastkernels}. Harness
Engineering preserves workload evidence during optimization
~\citep{shui2026harness}. Our focus is narrower. We study whether the evidence
supports promotion of one measured winner after candidate search.

Contract-Grade Verifier formalizes structural and behavioral checks
~\citep{shah2026contractgrade}. Correct but Slow shows why numerical validity
alone does not establish replacement quality~\citep{li2026correctslow}. Our
verifier enforces output structure, aliasing, mutation, special values, and
repeat execution. Distribution and precision oracles remain task specific.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The KernelBench study uses one Tesla T4, one model, three candidates per task,
and a deterministic feasible subset rather than a random sample. All valid tasks
are matrix multiplications. The result therefore characterizes this campaign,
not KernelBench L1 as a whole. The fused8 studies cover four tasks in one shape
regime. Their stress test and hardware control use one RTX A4500. Calibration
deliberately enriches candidates near parity, and the interval across four tasks
is wide. Seven confirmation processes provide a practical holdout, not a model
of tail behavior.

The fixed 2\% margin is a practical threshold declared before timing. It was not
calibrated to the observed noise. The multiplicity studies use deterministic
templates, so they answer a different question from candidate generation. We do
not analyze CUDA graphs, multiple GPUs, deployment cost, or model rankings. The
compiler baseline exists for 47 of 48 tasks, but only one selected winner receives
compiler confirmation in fresh processes.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
Plausible source and one fast timing sample do not establish a kernel speedup.
The protocol makes screening, confirmation, and evaluator validity separately
auditable. In this study, the external campaign supports no gain over eager. The
easy control shows a regime with no false promotion. The parity stress test
removes one screening winner, and the lifecycle study isolates a host timing
confound. Independent confirmation is a useful default even when the supported
result is a bound or a null finding.
